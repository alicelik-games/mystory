INFORMATION WE COLLECT

ACCOUNT INFORMATION

TwiWord requires account creation to access gameplay features. We collect:

  • Username chosen by you
  • Email address (via Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In)
  • Profile information provided during registration
  • Authentication tokens managed by Firebase Authentication

GAMEPLAY DATA

We collect information about how you play the game:

  • Game scores, levels, and achievements
  • Words played and daily challenge results
  • In-game currency balances (crystals, gold)
  • Skills, items, and inventory
  • Daily quest progress and completion
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • Friends list and social interactions
  • Online/offline presence status

TECHNICAL INFORMATION

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version and crash reports
  • IP address and country/region
  • Push notification tokens (FCM)

IN-APP PURCHASE INFORMATION

When you make in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive confirmation of purchase completion but do not collect or store payment card information. We record:

  • Purchase history and transaction IDs
  • Items purchased (crystal packs, gold packs, chests, special offers)
  • Purchase timestamps

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

GAME OPERATION

  • Operate and maintain game functionality
  • Sync your game progress across devices via Firebase
  • Provide leaderboard and social features
  • Process and deliver in-app purchases
  • Send push notifications for daily challenges and reminders

IMPROVEMENT AND ANALYTICS

  • Analyze gameplay patterns to improve game balance
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Develop new features based on user behavior

COMMUNICATION

  • Respond to support requests
  • Send game-related notifications (daily challenges, rewards)
  • Communicate important updates

DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

CLOUD STORAGE

Your game data is stored on Firebase/Firestore servers operated by Google. Data may be stored in data centers located in the United States or other countries.

LOCAL STORAGE

Some data is stored locally on your device:

  • Game settings and preferences
  • Word history (which words you have already played)
  • Cached game data for offline access

SECURITY MEASURES

  • All data transmissions use HTTPS encryption
  • Firebase security rules restrict data access
  • Authentication tokens are managed securely
  • No payment card data is stored on our servers

DATA RETENTION

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Gameplay Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Local Data: Stored until you delete the app
  • Support Communications: Deleted after resolving your inquiry (typically 90 days)

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not use TwiWord. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information should contact us at alicelik1980@gmail.com.

YOUR RIGHTS

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

To exercise your rights, contact us at alicelik1980@gmail.com.

ACCOUNT DELETION

To delete your account and associated data, contact us with "Delete My Account" in the subject line. We will process your request within 30 days.

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS

TwiWord sends push notifications for daily challenges, rewards, and game updates. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Notifications > TwiWord
  • Android: Settings > Apps > TwiWord > Notifications

INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

As we use Firebase (operated by Google), your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction, including the United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place through Google's standard contractual clauses.
