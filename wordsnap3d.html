Word Snap 3D

Find Words, Snap in 3D!

A Word Puzzle With Depth

Word Snap 3D takes classic word-search gameplay into three dimensions. Letters are scattered across a physical 3D space — rotate the scene, pick letters from any angle, drag them into the word tray and snap them together to reveal the hidden words.

🎯 Endless Levels: Rounds are generated continuously and scale in difficulty as you progress — word length, decoy letters and masking all ramp up with your level.

🧠 Target Words & Bonuses: Complete the level's target words, then keep going to find bonus words for extra rewards.

⚡ Skill Boosts: Five skills — including Magnet — that can be charged with gold or by watching an optional ad.

🎁 Daily Rewards & Challenges: A 7-day login streak, plus daily and weekly quests to keep the run going.

🧰 Chests & Currency: Earn coins and gems, open chests with fixed, fully transparent rewards.

👥 Friends & Leaderboards: Add friends by nickname, compare scores, and challenge them to daily, weekly or monthly word duels.

🏆 Achievements: Long-term goals that track your lifetime progress.

☁️ Optional Cloud Sync: Sign in with Apple or Google to carry your progress across devices — or play entirely offline without an account.

Relaxed by Design

There is no fail state and no timer pressure. You cannot lose a round — you can only find more words. Ads are never forced on you: every ad in the game is an optional, opt-in rewarded video that you choose to watch in exchange for a specific benefit.
