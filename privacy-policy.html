INFORMATION WE COLLECT FROM YOU

Most of the information that we collect about you comes directly from you when you play our Game or interact with third party ads in our Game. This information is collected through SDKs (Software Development Kits) which are technical tools integrated into our Game.

TECHNICAL INFORMATION

We collect technical information about your device that are necessary for you to use our Game and additional technical data that enables us to maintain and analyze the functioning of the Game, to personalize the content of the Game, and to display advertisements. Such information includes:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Persistent identifiers such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA)
  • Unique user ID specific for our Game
  • Country or region that you are playing in
  • Device performance and compatibility data

ADVERTISING AND GAME USAGE INFORMATION

When you use our Game, we collect information about:

  • How you play our Game (levels completed, time spent, game interactions)
  • How you interact with advertisements shown in our Game
  • Game progress, scores, and achievements
  • In-game settings and preferences (sound, haptic/vibration feedback)
  • Tutorial completion status
  • Special skills usage and purchases
  • Word formation patterns and vocabulary usage (anonymized)
  • Device performance during gameplay

GAME-SPECIFIC DATA COLLECTION

Our word puzzle game includes specific features that involve data processing:

Word Dictionary System:

  • The game uses pre-loaded word lists (CSV files) in multiple languages
  • No user-created words are collected or transmitted
  • Word validation is performed locally on your device
  • Vocabulary statistics are collected anonymously for game improvement

Local Data Storage (PlayerPrefs):

  • Game progress and level achievements
  • Sound and haptic feedback preferences
  • Special skills inventory and usage history
  • High scores and personal statistics
  • Language preference settings
  • Tutorial completion markers

Device Interaction Features:

  • Haptic Feedback: When enabled, the game triggers device vibration for enhanced gameplay experience
  • Audio Settings: Sound preferences are stored locally and not transmitted
  • Touch Input: Swipe and tap patterns are processed locally for game controls

CLASSICAL PERSONAL INFORMATION

We do not collect classical personal information through our Game (such as your name, address, email or phone number) and you can use our Game without providing us with your classical personal information. We will receive your classical personal information only when you contact us directly and will use such information only to respond to your inquiry.

ANALYTICS AND RESEARCH

DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

LOCAL DATA STORAGE

Most of your game data is stored locally on your device using Unity's PlayerPrefs system:

  • Game Progress: Levels completed, current level, total score
  • Settings: Sound preferences, haptic feedback settings, language selection
  • Achievements: Unlocked skills, special accomplishments, tutorial completion
  • Statistics: Personal gameplay metrics, high scores
  • Preferences: Audio/visual settings, control preferences

CLOUD DATA (LIMITED)

Only the following data may be transmitted for service functionality:

  • Anonymous usage analytics for game improvement
  • Crash reports for technical support (no personal data)
  • Advertising interaction data (handled by Google AdMob)

SECURITY MEASURES

  • Local data is stored securely using Unity's encrypted PlayerPrefs
  • Network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • No sensitive personal information is transmitted
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Access controls and data minimization practices

DATA RETENTION PERIODS

  • Local Game Data: Stored until you delete the app or clear app data
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized data retained for up to 24 months
  • Advertising Data: Retained according to Google AdMob policies
  • Support Communications: Deleted after resolving your inquiry (typically 90 days)

HAPTIC FEEDBACK PRIVACY

When you enable haptic feedback in game settings:

  • The game only triggers device vibration through standard system APIs
  • No data about haptic patterns or usage is collected
  • This feature can be disabled at any time in game settings
  • No personal biometric data is collected through haptic interactions
