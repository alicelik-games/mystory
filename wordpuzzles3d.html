Word Puzzles 3D

A 3D Word Puzzle Experience

Next-Gen Puzzle

Word Puzzles 3D brings classic word-search mechanics into the third dimension. Players rotate 3D objects to find letters hidden all around them and combine them into hidden words.

✅ 360° Control: Rotate the object to find letters from every angle.

✅ Brain Exercise: Sharpen your spatial reasoning and vocabulary at the same time.

✅ Visual Feast: Modern graphics and relaxing sound effects.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

As developer Ali Çelik, we care about our players' privacy. Word Puzzles 3D is a single-player word puzzle game. This policy explains the limited data the app works with and why. The app does not require you to create an account or sign in, does not show third-party ads, and does not sell your data.

1. No Account Required

You can play Word Puzzles 3D without registering, signing in, or providing a name or email address. The app does not create a user account for you and does not collect your contact details.

2. Game Progress (Stored on Your Device)

Your level progress, in-game coin/gem balance and settings (such as the sound on/off preference) are saved only on your own device using the operating system's local storage. This data is not uploaded to us or synced to any server, so it is not accessible to the developer. Removing the app deletes this data from your device.

3. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver reminders (for example, daily reward reminders). For this, a notification token for your device is processed by Firebase. You can turn notifications off at any time from your device settings; the app works fully without them.

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

The app includes Google Firebase, which may automatically collect limited, aggregated analytics and diagnostic information — such as app usage events, general device and operating-system information, and a Firebase app-instance identifier — to help us understand how the game is used and fix crashes. This information is not used to identify you personally and is not used for advertising.

5. No Advertising and No Tracking

Word Puzzles 3D does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising/tracking SDKs. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not build an advertising profile about you.

6. In-App Items

Coins and gems in the game are virtual items that are earned and spent entirely within the game. They have no real-world monetary value and are not purchased with real money.

7. Data Sharing

Data processed by the app (the notification token and Firebase analytics/diagnostics information) is handled only by Google/Firebase, acting as our service provider (data processor), over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We never sell your data and do not share it with other third parties for their own purposes.

8. International Data Transfers

Google/Firebase may process and store the above information on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, under their own standard contractual safeguards.

9. Data Retention

On-device data (progress, coin/gem balance, settings) remains on your device for as long as the app is installed. The notification token is retained by Firebase while notifications are enabled. Firebase analytics/diagnostics data is retained according to Google's standard retention periods.

10. Children

The app is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your Rights (GDPR / EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If data protection law such as the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access the limited data processed about you, request correction or erasure, request a portable copy, and object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below; we will respond within a reasonable time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

12. Your Rights Under Turkish Law (KVKK)

The developer of this app is based in Türkiye, so the processing of your personal data is also subject to Türkiye's Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 ("KVKK"), independently of and in addition to any other law that may apply to you (such as the GDPR above).

Data Controller: Ali Çelik, developer of Word Puzzles 3D. Contact: the email address in Section 13 below.

Legal grounds for processing (KVKK Art. 5-6): the notification token is processed based on your explicit consent (you choose to allow notifications); analytics/diagnostics are processed based on our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the game.

Cross-border transfer: our service provider (Google/Firebase) processes data on servers located outside Türkiye. This transfer relies on the safeguards recognized under KVKK Art. 9 and/or your explicit consent.

Your rights (KVKK Art. 11): you may request to learn whether your personal data is being processed and obtain information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether your data is used accordingly; learn the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom your data is transferred; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request deletion or destruction of your data where the grounds for processing no longer apply, and request that such correction/deletion be notified to any third party your data was transferred to; object to a result to your detriment arising solely from automated analysis of your data; and request compensation if you are harmed by unlawful processing.

How to apply: submit your request in writing to the contact e-mail below, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. We will respond within 30 days as required by KVKK Art. 13. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

13. Contact and Data Requests

For any privacy question, or to request deletion of the notification token or any Firebase data associated with your device, contact us at alicelik1980@gmail.com. Since the app stores game progress only on your device, you can remove that data yourself at any time by uninstalling the app.
