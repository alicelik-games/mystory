Word Puzzles 3D

A 3D Word Puzzle Experience

Next-Gen Puzzle

Word Puzzles 3D brings classic word-search mechanics into the third dimension. Players rotate 3D objects to find letters hidden all around them and combine them into hidden words.

✅ 360° Control: Rotate the object to find letters from every angle.

✅ Brain Exercise: Sharpen your spatial reasoning and vocabulary at the same time.

✅ Visual Feast: Modern graphics and relaxing sound effects.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

As developer Ali Çelik, we care about our players' privacy. Below is an explanation of what data the app collects and why.

1. Account and Sign-In

The app optionally offers Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. When you sign in, a unique user identifier and (if you choose to share it) your name/email address are obtained from the provider you selected. With Apple sign-in, you can use the "Hide My Email" option.

2. Game Progress and Cloud Sync

Once signed in, your level progress, coin/gem balance, achievements, and nickname are securely stored on Google Firebase so they can sync across your devices. If you play without signing in, your progress is kept only on your device.

3. Friends and Leaderboard

If you use the friends, challenges, or leaderboard features, your nickname and score may be visible to the friends you've added and/or to other players viewing the leaderboard.

4. Push Notifications

To deliver notifications such as friend challenges and daily reward reminders, a notification token for your device is stored. You can turn off notifications at any time from your device settings.

5. Advertising and Tracking Permission

The app uses Google AdMob for rewarded video ads. On iOS devices, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission is requested in order to show personalized ads; declining this permission does not prevent you from playing the game or earning rewards by watching ads — ads will simply be less personalized.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app additionally shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting any ad, in line with the GDPR and Google's EU User Consent Policy. You choose whether personalized or only non-personalized ads may be shown to you, and you can change this choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Choices inside the app.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed directly through the Apple App Store / Google Play; your card or payment details are never seen or stored by the developer. Purchase receipts are validated server-side only to credit the purchased content to your account.

7. Data Security and Sharing

Data is transmitted and stored via encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) on Google Firebase infrastructure. Your data is never sold; it is shared only with the service providers necessary to run the app (Google/Firebase, Google AdMob, the Apple/Google stores), acting as our data processors.

8. Data Retention

Account and cloud-sync data (progress, coin/gem balance, achievements, nickname, friends, notification token) is kept for as long as your account exists. If you don't sign in, on-device data is kept only for as long as the app remains installed. Deleting your account (see below) removes this data from our systems.

9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers (Google/Firebase, Google AdMob) may process and store data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, under their own standard contractual safeguards.

10. Your Rights (GDPR / EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If data protection law such as the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to: access the data we hold about you, correct inaccurate data, request erasure of your data, request a copy of your data in a portable format, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent for ad personalization at any time (see Section 5). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below; we will respond within a reasonable time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

11. Your Rights Under Turkish Law (KVKK)

The developer of this app is based in Türkiye, so the processing of your personal data is also subject to Türkiye's Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 ("KVKK"), independently of and in addition to any other law that may apply to you (such as the GDPR above).

Data Controller: Ali Çelik, developer of Word Puzzles 3D / Word Snap 3D. Contact: the email address in Section 12 below.

Legal grounds for processing (KVKK Art. 5-6): your data is processed based on your explicit consent (e.g., optional sign-in, ad personalization) and, where applicable, because processing is necessary to perform the specific service you requested (e.g., syncing your progress once you sign in).

Cross-border transfer: our service providers (Google/Firebase, Google AdMob) process data on servers located outside Türkiye. This transfer is based on your explicit consent and/or the safeguards recognized under KVKK Art. 9.

Your rights (KVKK Art. 11): you may request to learn whether your personal data is being processed and obtain information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether your data is used accordingly; learn the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom your data is transferred; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request deletion or destruction of your data where the grounds for processing no longer apply, and request that such correction/deletion be notified to any third party your data was transferred to; object to a result that is to your detriment arising solely from the automated analysis of your data; and request compensation if you are harmed by unlawful processing.

How to apply: submit your request in writing to the contact e-mail below, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. We will respond within 30 days as required by KVKK Art. 13. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu).

12. Deleting Your Data

To request deletion of your account and any data stored in the cloud, please contact us at the email address below.
